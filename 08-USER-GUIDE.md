# RAJA ELECTRONICS — Customer User Guide

## 1. Browse Products
Open the Shop area to browse products by category.

Use search, filters and sorting to find products quickly.

## 2. Product Details
Select a product to view:
- Product name
- Brand
- Price
- Availability
- Specifications
- Warranty
- EMI information where available

## 3. Cart
Add products to the cart and adjust quantities before checkout.

## 4. Wishlist & Compare
Use Wishlist to save products for later.

Use Compare to evaluate multiple products side by side.

## 5. Billing
Enter customer billing information and review:
- Product subtotal
- GST
- Discounts
- Coupon discount
- Delivery charge
- Installation/technician fee
- Grand total

## 6. EMI
Choose EMI when available and enter the required finance details.

The application displays an EMI summary before payment.

## 7. Payment
Select the available payment method and complete the payment flow.

For a production deployment, payment completion is confirmed by the payment gateway/backend.

## 8. Invoice
After successful payment, the invoice can be viewed.

The invoice includes:
- Invoice number
- Date/time
- Customer information
- GST information
- Items
- Tax
- Discounts
- Charges
- Grand total
- QR code
- Barcode
- EMI information when applicable

The invoice supports printing and PDF generation.

## 9. Orders
Customers can view order status and follow delivery progress.

## 10. Warranty & Support
Use the available service/support functions to access warranty information and customer assistance.

## 11. Accessibility
The application includes keyboard focus styling, reduced-motion support, mobile touch-target improvements and audio/voice-related functionality.
